# Platform Adaptation

## Decision rule

Choose a platform only when audience, objective, content form, production capacity, and conversion path reasonably fit. Do not use platform stereotypes as fixed rules.

## Adaptation matrix

| Platform | Typical user task to test | Adaptation focus |
|---|---|---|
| Douyin | Discover or be entertained quickly | Immediate reason to watch, visible process, concise payoff |
| Video Accounts | Share or discuss within relationships | Practical relevance, trust, family/work context, clear share value |
| Xiaohongshu | Search, compare, save, or decide | Searchable framing, lived evidence, tradeoffs, useful detail |
| Kuaishou | Follow a person, process, or direct value | Direct language, authentic person/process, strong feedback loop |
| Bilibili | Understand deeply or follow a niche | Evidence, context, reasoning, structure, creator point of view |
| WeChat Official Account | Read, retain, or act after reflection | Complete narrative, proof, depth, durable conversion path |

These are hypotheses. Validate them with the account's audience, current platform behavior, and results.

## Rebuild, do not repost

For each chosen platform specify: audience job, one content angle, hook, body structure, format/length range, visual treatment, CTA, and success metric. Reuse source footage only if the resulting post still fits the platform objective.

## Suitability output

Classify each platform as `primary`, `test`, `repurpose only`, or `not recommended now`, with a one-sentence reason.
