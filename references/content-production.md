# Content Production

## Build pillars from decision needs

Use only pillars supported by real material:

- customer problem or context;
- choice criteria and tradeoffs;
- objection or risk explanation;
- process and judgment evidence;
- bounded result evidence;
- authentic person, story, or values;
- comparison, demonstration, or experiment.

Avoid generic self-praise. Convert “we are professional” into a verifiable process, standard, demonstration, or customer-relevant observation.

## Per-post asset contract

When requested, provide: objective, audience, evidence used, title options, first-3-second hook, beat-by-beat script, on-screen text, cover text, caption, CTA, comment prompt, shot list, props/locations/people, edit notes, and required approvals. Omit any asset the user does not need.

## Direction approval gate

When there are two or more possible directions, use two phases:

1. `Choose`: compare each direction using only concept, audience need, content mechanism, evidence required, production difficulty, risk, and recommendation. End by asking the user to select one direction or request replacements.
2. `Produce`: after explicit selection or approval, create the complete execution pack for the approved direction only: titles, hook, beat-by-beat script, shots, on-screen text, caption, CTA, BGM specification, props, schedule, approvals, risks, and measurement plan.

A list of several post topics inside one already-approved direction is not a new strategy choice. It may be delivered as a batch when the user requests a content calendar or series.

## Production feasibility

Constrain output to available people, locations, budget, and time. For a one-person phone setup, prefer one location, natural sound or simple voiceover, repeatable shots, and a limited batch. State what cannot be responsibly produced within the constraint.

When people, budget, or time are explicitly constrained, provide a minimum viable output count, a shooting/editing/publishing schedule within the stated period, an upper-bound budget allocation, and a `not doing` list. Do not allocate budget to paid promotion until the current settings and account eligibility are verified.

## Claims

Show conditions, source, sample limitations, and timeframe for result claims. Never create fake customer stories, before/after results, scarcity, or testimonials.
