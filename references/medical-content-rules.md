# Medical Content Rules for China Platforms

Last verified: 2026-08-12. This is an operational checklist, not legal advice. Laws and platform rules change; verify the official source and the publishing account's current backend before every release.

## Classify before writing

Classify the intended post before drafting:

1. `institution information`: objective, legally supportable public information without promotion;
2. `independent health education`: scientific information without institution/service/product promotion or linked conversion;
3. `medical institution or service advertising`: direct or indirect promotion of a clinic, hospital, department, practitioner service, treatment, booking, discount, or consultation;
4. `medical-device advertising`: promotion containing a device and its use, scope, mechanism, performance, or purchase path;
5. `medical-professional content`: professional medical education produced under a physician or other medical-worker identity.

Apply national law first, then any stricter verified platform rule. Do not combine the safer permissions of different categories.

## Stop gates

| Intended content | Evidence required before a promotional draft | Stop when missing |
|---|---|---|
| Medical institution/service advertising | lawful institution status, approved practice scope, valid `医疗广告审查证明`, approved final sample and media type | Do not draft service promotion, disease/treatment claims, booking, discounts, private-message lead capture, or calls to visit |
| Medical-device advertising | registration/filing and instructions, lawful advertiser/authorization, applicable production/operation qualification, valid advertising approval and approved sample | Do not draft promotion, product link, shopping-cart plan, physician/patient endorsement, or claims beyond the approved documents |
| Professional medical education | creator identity and professional qualification, platform medical certification where required, professional scope, employer reporting/approval where applicable, sources and patient consent | Do not present the creator as a medical professional or produce specialist advice under that identity |
| Patient story or clinical material | specific written authorization, minimum necessary disclosure, factual source, institutional approval | Do not use identifiable images, records, names, voices, test results, or fabricated/composite cases |

## National baseline

### Medical institutions and services

Under the `医疗广告管理办法`, medical advertising includes direct or indirect introduction of a medical institution or medical service. Before publication:

- only a lawful medical institution may publish it; an internal department cannot advertise in its own name;
- obtain a valid `医疗广告审查证明` and publish only the approved sample in its approved media category;
- display the institution's first registered name and the review-certificate number;
- do not add medical technologies, treatment methods, disease names, drugs, cure guarantees, cure/effective rates, comparisons, disparagement, or patient/medical-worker/research endorsements;
- do not disguise the advertising as news, interviews, medical information, or health education.

The `医疗广告认定指南` distinguishes objective information disclosure from promotion. Treat subjective praise of the environment, equipment, techniques, process, safety, efficacy, discounts, institution comparisons, case-based promotion, service links, and related product links as an advertising risk.

### Internet health education

Under the `互联网广告管理办法`:

- medical, drug, medical-device and other review-required advertising must be reviewed before publication and must not be edited, spliced or changed from the approved content;
- do not use health or wellness education to disguise medical, drug, device, health-food, or special-medical-food advertising;
- do not show the related provider's address, contact details or shopping link on the same page or at the same time as that health education;
- clearly identify advertising when knowledge, experience or review content promotes a product or service and adds a purchase route, unless the advertising is prohibited altogether;
- do not publish medical or medical-device advertising in internet media directed at minors.

### Medical devices

Use the current `药品、医疗器械、保健食品、特殊医学用途配方食品广告审查管理暂行办法`, effective 2020-03-01:

- advertising requires prior review and must stay within the registration/filing documents and approved instructions;
- show the advertising approval number; required warnings must remain clear and continuously visible in video;
- a personal-use device requires the statutory instruction to read the instructions or use it under medical guidance; show the statutory contraindication/precaution notice when applicable;
- prohibit state/military endorsement; physician, pharmacist, expert, academic, association or patient recommendation; all-disease/all-person claims; fear marketing; `安全`, `无毒副作用`, natural-means-safe claims; hot-sale, rush-buy, free treatment/gift, ranking, award, guaranteed refund, or necessity claims;
- do not place a medical institution's name, address, contact details, services, treatment method, free clinic, consultation number, or special clinic inside a medical-device advertisement.

Do not cite the 2009 medical-device advertising rules as current law: the 2020 interim measures repealed them. The 2026 replacement is still a consultation draft as of the verification date.

### Physicians and other medical workers

The 2025 four-authority notice on self-media medical education requires platforms to verify medical qualifications and prohibits unqualified accounts from publishing professional medical education. Require accurate identity, current employment/scope and source or AI labels as applicable.

The `医务人员互联网健康科普负面行为清单（试行）` prohibits:

- disguised advertising, referral or lead routing, and live commerce for medical products, wellness courses or health food;
- patient-information disclosure or identifiable patient material without authorization;
- content outside the worker's role or professional scope;
- unverified science, false information, or distorted guidelines;
- exaggerating illness or efficacy, `神医/神药`, fabricated cases or stories;
- unverified or unlabelled AI-generated medical education;
- vulgar, suggestive or sensational traffic tactics;
- using an institution or professional identity without reporting it to the employer; or retaining a former employer/title after leaving.

## Platform overlays

Platform rules supplement, not replace, national law. Record the exact rule, scope, version/date and account type checked.

### Douyin

- Publicly accessible official pages found during this audit include older local-services/e-commerce notices, but a stable current public text for ordinary medical community content was not verified.
- Historical notices must not be presented as the 2026 full category or qualification list.
- Before publication or commerce activation, verify in the current Douyin rule center/account backend: medical category availability, institution and practitioner certification, `医疗广告审查证明`, approved sample, conversion tools, product-link eligibility, and paid-promotion eligibility.
- Until verified, apply the national stop gates and do not infer that a visible posting, group-buying or advertising feature authorizes medical promotion.

### Kuaishou

- The official local-life qualification page lists Chinese medicine as invitation-only and requires a medical institution licence; medical advertising requires a `医疗广告审查证明`. Medical-device sales qualifications depend on device class.
- The official local-life creator guide prohibits inaccurate price/package information, low-price bait, absolute/ranking/scarcity language and fabricated or exaggerated service efficacy.
- Public page dates and the ordinary medical-community-content rule were not fully verified. Recheck the current merchant/creator backend before publication.

### Xiaohongshu

- The verified `蒲公英内容审核规范` applies to commercial collaboration notes, not automatically to every organic note.
- For that scope it prohibits cure/effect/safety guarantees, `100%有效`, no-risk/no-side-effect, immediate/permanent effect, fabricated inherited-secret narratives, disguised personal-experience promotion, false institutional endorsement, and medical marketing through physician imagery or first-person physician presentation in specified commercial-account contexts.
- It restricts unqualified medical terminology and medical-service promotion, physician accounts promoting drugs/medical devices/health food/special medical food, indirect merchant routing and non-medical products using disease-treatment claims or hospital/pharmacy settings to imply medical properties.
- Recheck the current organic-note, professional-account, store, live and commercial-collaboration rules separately; do not generalize the collaboration rule beyond its scope.

### WeChat Channels and Official Accounts

- No stable publicly accessible current Tencent medical-special-rule text was verified in this audit. Do not cite third-party reproductions as Tencent rules.
- Apply the national baseline and verify inside the current WeChat rule center: account/medical certification, live-stream identity, external linking, consultation or booking, store/product eligibility, paid promotion and health-education requirements.

### Bilibili

- The verified official professional-certification rule requires the account owner, credential holder and on-camera person to match, and the certified field to match the content. Physician certification requires specified identity, national health-registry, practice, qualification, department and title evidence.
- Personal professional certification does not substitute for institutional-account certification.
- An official medicine-category page indicates medical education requires medical education or physician certification and prohibits impersonating a physician and commercial promotion, but the full current page could not be re-read because of access verification. Confirm the current upload-category rule in the publishing interface.
- The public text of Bilibili's current high-risk-industry commercial-admission rule was not fully verified. Confirm medical-institution and device advertising eligibility with the commercial backend or written platform support before planning paid distribution.

## Required pre-publish output

For regulated medical content, include a release checklist with:

1. classification and why;
2. national legal basis and official URLs checked;
3. platform rule name, scope and version/date checked;
4. account identity and qualification evidence;
5. advertising-review document, approval number, approved sample and media-category match;
6. claim-to-evidence mapping;
7. patient, staff, music, footage and location permissions;
8. advertising label, statutory warnings and AI/source labels;
9. conversion-path check: link, booking, address, phone, private message, group buying, store or shopping cart;
10. final status: `publishable`, `publish after correction`, `blocked`, or `platform rule unverified`.

When any current platform rule is inaccessible, state `platform rule unverified`, give the exact backend/support item to check, and continue only with non-promotional portions that satisfy national law.
