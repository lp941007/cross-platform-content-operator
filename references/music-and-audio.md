# Music and Audio

## Specify before naming

Define content function, target emotion, tempo/energy, vocal density, voice priority, entry cue, shift cue, ending, and licensing need. BGM supports comprehension and pacing; it does not guarantee distribution.

## Content-function starting points

| Function | Audio direction |
|---|---|
| Story | Emotional continuity; leave room for narrative turns |
| Viewpoint/knowledge | Low-distraction bed; speech remains dominant |
| Demonstration/card point | Clear beat and edit markers |
| Comedy | Space for timing, contrast, and sound effects |
| Motivation | Controlled rise that matches an earned payoff |

## Exact tracks

Before recommending a named track, verify current platform availability, commercial-use status, cross-platform rights, and any territorial restrictions. Give a style fallback: mood, BPM range, instruments, and vocal density. Do not imply that a track is licensed merely because it is popular or visible in a platform library.
