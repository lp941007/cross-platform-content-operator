# Output Contract

Scale the deliverable to the request. Use only applicable sections, in this order:

1. Brief and evidence status
2. Missing information and assumptions
3. Platform suitability and objective
4. Content pillars and strategy
5. Platform-specific content assets
6. Production and audio plan
7. Publishing, test, and measurement plan
8. Compliance and execution risks

For every recommendation, make the evidence status visible. If the user asks for a single caption, give the caption plus only the critical assumption and risk note; do not force a full strategy document.

For multi-platform requests, distinguish the shared factual/source-material master from each platform's native asset. A shared title, script, cover, and CTA do not count as completed cross-platform adaptation.

## Decision-ready format

Use concise tables where several platforms need equivalent fields. Mark actions as `do now`, `test`, `verify before use`, or `needs user input`.
