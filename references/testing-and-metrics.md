# Testing and Metrics

## Test design

Set one primary objective per test: attention, qualified interaction, account growth, lead, store visit, or sale. Establish a baseline, test a limited variable set, define a budget/time ceiling, and decide scale/stop/revise conditions before launch.

## Metrics by objective

| Objective | Primary measures |
|---|---|
| Attention | Early retention, average watch time, completion where relevant |
| Interaction | Qualified comments, saves, shares, meaningful reply rate |
| Growth | Profile visits, follow conversion, audience relevance |
| Lead | Valid inquiry rate, form completion, cost per qualified lead |
| Store visit | Reservation, coupon/ticket use, verified store visit |
| Sale | Conversion, acquisition cost, gross margin, repeat purchase |

Do not optimize only for views when the objective is lead or sale.

## Guaranteed-result requests

Explicitly decline any request to guarantee volume, leads, or sales. Define a qualified lead, baseline, budget ceiling, test period, and acceptable acquisition cost before proposing a test objective and scale condition. Do not repeat a guarantee in titles, scripts, CTAs, or promotion instructions.

## Paid promotion

Use a small-budget test only after confirming current platform settings and policies. Do not prescribe fixed spend, timing, audience setting, or retention thresholds across industries. Report the baseline, the tested variable, observed result, decision, and next iteration.

If the promotion dashboard, policy, or account eligibility cannot be verified live, do not give specific setting values or claim current availability. List the items to verify, the official verification source, and a non-paid content test that can proceed first.
